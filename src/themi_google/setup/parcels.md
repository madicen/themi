# Parcels

Themi keeps track of parcels from shipping emails: one per tracking number, so the shop's
"shipped" email and the carrier's "out for delivery" are the same parcel on the wall.

The carriers send free emails of their own about every parcel coming to your address,
including ones from shops that never email a tracking number. Signing up for them gives
Themi more to go on. Sign up with an email address Themi reads (one linked on the
[Accounts page](/accounts) with email triage on):

1. **USPS Informed Delivery**, at
   [informeddelivery.usps.com](https://informeddelivery.usps.com): a morning email listing
   the packages coming that day and soon.
2. **UPS My Choice**, at [ups.com/mychoice](https://www.ups.com/mychoice): an email when a
   UPS parcel is on its way, out for delivery (with a two-hour window) and delivered.
3. **FedEx Delivery Manager**, at
   [fedex.com/delivery-manager](https://www.fedex.com/en-us/delivery-manager.html): the
   same for FedEx.

Each is free. Themi never needs your passwords for them: it only reads the emails they
send. The wall shows only the shop's name and when a parcel is due, never what's in it.
