# The Cozyla wall's chores and routines

With this on, Themi runs the wall's chore chart: its chores are written there and kept that
way, and a tick on either side counts on both. It reads the wall's routines too, and asks
about the ones not done yet.

It uses the same Cozyla account as the to-do lists, and the same unofficial way in, which
may change or break.

1. Enter the family's **Cozyla account email** and **password**, if they aren't saved yet.
2. Turn on **Keep the wall's chore chart in step**.
3. Save, and press **Test**.

Change chores in Themi, not on the wall: an edit on the wall is put back.
