# The Cozyla wall's to-do lists

Themi can keep the Cozyla wall calendar's to-do lists in step with its tasks, both ways.

Cozyla has no official way in. This uses the one its tablet apps use, which may change or
break, and may go against Cozyla's terms. Use the family's own account.

1. Enter the family's **Cozyla account email** and **password**.
2. Enter the name of the main to-do list, where Themi's tasks go. Leave it blank to keep
   the lists out of step.
3. Save, and press **Test**: Themi signs in, changes nothing, and reads the lists.

Members are matched by first name. A to-do assigned to the family as a whole is shared:
either of you can tick it off.
