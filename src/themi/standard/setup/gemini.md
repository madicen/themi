# Use Google's Gemini

Gemini is the easiest way to start: its free tier is enough for a family, and it's the
first choice for everyone's replies.

Turning this on makes Gemini available. Each person then adds a key of their own, so
nobody's use counts against anyone else's.

1. Go to [Google AI Studio](https://aistudio.google.com/apikey) and sign in with a Google
   account.
2. Press **Create API key** and copy it.
3. In Themi, open [My AI](/ai) and paste the key into Gemini.

Each person who wants their own key does the same on their own My AI page.
