# Drawings

With this on, Themi draws pictures from its own data: a picture of the day with the
morning message (today's events at their times, the weather beside them, and a quiet sign
of what's waiting), and, when someone asks, the week ahead, the weather and more.

It needs no account and no key, and nothing leaves the house: the pictures are drawn on
Themi's own computer, never by an AI picture model.

1. Turn this on and save.
2. Press **Test** to draw one small picture.

Each person can turn the morning picture off, or change how it looks, on their Settings
page.
