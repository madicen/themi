# The weather at home

With this on, the morning message gets one line about the weather when the day's weather
is worth knowing: rain, snow, a storm, a freeze, real heat or cold, strong wind. On a fair
day there's no line.

It needs no account and no key. The forecast comes from
[Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com), which is free for a household. In the US, official
warnings come from the [National Weather Service](https://www.weather.gov).

1. Set the home address on the [Family page](/family), if it isn't set yet.
2. Turn this on and save.
3. Press **Test** to fetch one forecast.

The units are °F, inches and mph unless you choose °C. The advanced settings hold what
counts as worth knowing (how likely rain must be, how hot is hot, and so on).
