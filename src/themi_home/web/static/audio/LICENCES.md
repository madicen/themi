# Built-in loops for the session page (BD5a)

Every loop here is original and dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0
(https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). No recording from anywhere else is
in them except the instrument samples named below, played into new music.

- Rain and brown noise are synthesised from code by `tools/make_loops.py` (ffmpeg's noise
  sources).
- Lofi and soft piano (F48a) are composed from a seed by `tools/compose_loops.py` and played
  on sampled instruments:
  - the Versilian Community Sample Library (VCSL), CC0 1.0: the Kawai grand piano, hi-hats
    and snare (https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL);
  - jRhodes3d by Jeff Learman, a 1977 Rhodes Mark I. Its licence makes music made with it
    CC0 ("License for musicians using this to make music: CC0"); the samples themselves are
    CC BY-NC 4.0 and are not included here
    (https://github.com/sfzinstruments/jlearman.jRhodes3d).
  The kick and the bass are synthesised. Nothing imitates a record: no crackle or hiss.

## Themi's trills (WA4)

The wall's alert sounds in `trills/`, synthesised from code by `tools/make_trills.py` (ffmpeg
alone: a swept sine with a fast vibrato and a little second harmonic; the notes are in
`themi_home/trills.py`). Original, and dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0.
