# Music from Spotify

During a body double session, Themi can play music that suits the task, from each person's
own Spotify.

1. At [developer.spotify.com/dashboard](https://developer.spotify.com/dashboard), make an
   app with the **Web API**.
2. Add this redirect address: `$SITE/auth/spotify/callback`
3. Paste the app's **client ID** here and save. Spotify gives a secret too; Themi doesn't
   need it.

## After the restart

Each person who wants it links their Spotify on [Accounts](/accounts).
