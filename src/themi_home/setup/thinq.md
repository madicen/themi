# The washer and dryer, through LG ThinQ

LG washers and dryers on the ThinQ app can tell Themi when they run and finish, so someone
gets a nudge to move the load. Themi only reads them. There's nothing to fill in here.

## After the restart

1. At [connect-pat.lgthinq.com](https://connect-pat.lgthinq.com), sign in with the LG
   account the appliances are on.
2. Add a personal access token with **View all devices** and **View all device statuses**.
3. On [Accounts](/accounts), an admin pastes it under **Washer and dryer**, with the
   country it was made for, and sets which appliance is the washer and which the dryer.
