# The wall

A page for the frame on the wall (the Cozyla): today's timeline, each of you and what you
share side by side, and the week ahead, on one screen that changes with the time of day.
From bedtime to the first morning routine it goes dim and shows tomorrow's plan.

The frame signs in as the household, not as a person, and can open only the wall.

1. Turn this on and save, then restart.
2. On a phone, open [Wall frames](/wall/frames) and press **Pair a frame**.
3. On the frame, open Chrome at Themi's address followed by `/wall/pair`, and type the code.
4. In Chrome's menu, choose **Install app** (or **Add to Home screen**, then **Install** if
   it offers both). The wall then opens from its own icon, full screen, with no address
   bar. Put the icon on Cozyla's home screen.

[Wall frames](/wall/frames) says how each frame shows the wall. **Full screen app** is the
install working. **In a Chrome tab** or **Full screen after a touch** means the frame has no
Google Play, so Chrome made a plain shortcut. That still works: the wall goes full screen
at its first touch, but it's back to Chrome's bars after a restart until it's touched
again. For no bars at all, even after a restart, use a kiosk browser (for example Fully
Kiosk Browser) with Themi's address and `/wall/pair` as its start page, and pair the frame
again in it (it keeps its own sign-in). Android 11 and later ignore the older adb
"immersive mode" setting, so that won't help.

While it's open, the wall keeps the screen on; Cozyla's own sleep schedule still turns it
off at night. If Themi restarts or the Wi-Fi drops, the frame keeps its last screen, marked
"Updated" with the time, and catches up by itself.

**Bedtime** (below) is when the night view starts; 11 pm unless you change it.

**Look** (below): light or dark. **Follow the frame** (the default) uses the frame's own
dark theme from its Android settings; **Dark in the evening** goes dark when the evening
routine starts and light again at the first morning routine. A touch at night always
shows the dark look.

**Deliveries** (below, on unless you turn it off): Themi's note on the wall mentions parcels
due or delivered today, from the shipping emails in watched inboxes. It names only the shop,
never what's inside, but around birthdays you may still want it off.

A frame stays signed in. **Sign this frame out** on Wall frames stops it at once.
