# The robot vacuum, through Roborock

A Roborock vacuum can tell Themi when it's stuck, full or out of water, so someone gets a
nudge. Roborock has no official way in, so this may stop working when Roborock changes
things. There's nothing to fill in here.

## After the restart

On [Accounts](/accounts), an admin enters the Roborock app's email under **Robot vacuum**,
presses **Send code**, and enters the code Roborock emails.
