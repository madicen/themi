# Pet feeders, through SmartLife

Feeders on the SmartLife or Tuya Smart app can tell Themi when food runs low or gets stuck.
Themi only reads them; it never feeds. There's nothing to fill in here.

## After the restart

1. Sign up at [platform.tuya.com](https://platform.tuya.com) and make a cloud project:
   **Smart Home**, in the data centre your app account is in.
2. In the project's **Devices** tab, choose **Link App Account** and scan its code from the
   SmartLife app.
3. Copy the project's **Access ID** and **Access Secret**. On [Accounts](/accounts), an
   admin pastes them under **Pet feeders**.

Tuya's free plan lasts a month at a time. Extending it on Tuya's site is free; Themi says
when it lapses.
