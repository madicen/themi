Connect the Safeway helper to Themi

The popup then shows Themi's grocery list, and "fill the Safeway cart" in Themi's chat fills it here.

  1. On Themi's website, open Safeway and click Connect the Safeway helper.
  2. Paste Themi's address and the token it shows below, then click Connect.
  3. Allow access to that address when Chrome asks.
